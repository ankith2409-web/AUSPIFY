THE IDEA

An emergency interface should never leave someone guessing.

THE SAVIOUR explores how a patient, a hospital, and traffic coordination could see the next step in one understandable journey. The current work is a design prototype. Any real service would require verified partners, secure infrastructure, and rigorous testing.
