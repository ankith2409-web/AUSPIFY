# Task 6 — research and evaluation protocol

**Status:** prepared, not conducted. Participants: **0**. No observations, quotes, or success rates are claimed. This is separate from internal browser and state-model QA.

## Initial user study

Plan five adult participants with varied phone confidence and language familiarity for a formative comprehension round. Obtain consent, anonymize IDs, and use an invented scenario and sample details only. Do not stage a dangerous incident or contact emergency services. Ask people to think aloud. Do not explain a state before asking what it means. Record assistance and uncertainty.

| ID | Neutral prompt | Success/observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | You are helping someone near a sample gate. Prepare a request, inspect another workspace, then return. | Location/context survive; participant understands this is a demonstration |
| R2 | Review the information, then examine the “not sent” screen. What happened and what could you do? | Distinguishes delivery failure from waiting; finds retry or direct-call alternative |
| R3 | Examine “request sent.” What has and has not happened? | Understands no provider or ambulance is assigned yet |
| R4 | Examine “no response.” What would you do next? | Understands no acceptance or ETA; recognizes the call alternative without making a call |
| R5 | In the local role demo, accept and dispatch a sample vehicle, acknowledge the route and mark arrival. Return to Patient. | Can explain the handoffs and locate the retained completed details |
| R6 | Complete another handoff without traffic acknowledgement. What does arrival prove? | Does not infer acknowledgement; sees absence explicitly |

Record independent/assisted/failed outcome, task duration, errors, actual quote, interpretation of the current status, and ease from 1–7. Use participant-results.csv. Report counts and denominators; do not generalize small-sample results to emergency performance. Proposed acceptance: no participant mistakes the prototype for a live emergency service, all observed participants distinguish waiting from acceptance, and no blocker remains in the tested recovery paths. These are targets, not achieved results.

## Staff discovery before service validation

Separately plan consented interviews with authorized hospital coordinators and traffic staff. Ask how intake, verification, dispatch, route decisions and closure work today; who owns each handoff; what information is needed; and which proposed actions would be inappropriate. Request no patient records or operationally sensitive details. Map contradictory feedback, especially if the traffic-role premise is rejected. There are no staff interviews in this submission.

## Synthesis and iteration

Group observed errors by state, ownership and recovery. Label blocker/major/minor, link each issue to an actual observation, revise, then retest affected tasks with fresh participants where feasible. Track draft → wireframe → screen → observation → revision. Do not replace user findings with agent self-review.
